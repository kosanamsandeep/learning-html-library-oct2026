2. 2026 Technique Evaluation — Original Repo vs. State-of-the-Art

Comparison of the techniques used across the source repo against what is considered best-practice in mid-2026.

AreaOriginal Repo Technique2026 SOTAStatusRecommendation
EmbeddingsOpenAI text-embedding-3, Gemini Embedding 2 Matryoshka + late-interaction (ColBERT-v2 / ColPali, NV-Embed-v3, Voyage-3-large) Acceptable Move to ColPali for visual docs; Matryoshka for cost-tunable dim.
