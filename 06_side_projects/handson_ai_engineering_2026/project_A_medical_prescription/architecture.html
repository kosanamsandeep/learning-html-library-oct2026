Project A — Medical Prescription Digitizer

Turns a photo of a prescription into a validated Prescription JSON, cross-checks every drug against RxNorm, and stores it in a normalized SQLite schema.

1 · End-to-End Architecture

flowchart LR IMG[📷 Prescription Image] --> EXT[PrescriptionExtractor
GPT-5-Vision] EXT -->|response_format=Pydantic| RX[Prescription dataclass] RX --> VAL[RxNormValidator
RxNav REST] VAL -->|rxnorm_cui| RX2[Validated Prescription] RX2 --> PERS[SQLite Persister] PERS --> DB[(prescriptions.db)] RX2 --> API[(JSON output)]

2 · Component Table
