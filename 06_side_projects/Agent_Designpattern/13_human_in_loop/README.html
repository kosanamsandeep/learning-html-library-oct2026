Chapter 13 — Human-in-the-Loop

The agent pauses for a human to approve, edit, or veto before sensitive actions.

Approval gateChain of ResponsibilityState

What is it?

For high-risk operations (payments, code merges, medical advice, legal text) you bake in checkpoints where a human must confirm. The agent serializes its plan, asks, and resumes (or aborts) on the response.

Mental model: a junior banker who can prepare paperwork but can't sign — partner approval required.

Pattern flow

flowchart TD R([Request]) --> A[Agent processes] A --> S{Risk score > threshold?} S -->|"low"| AUTO[Auto-approve] S -->|"high"| H[[Human reviewer]] H -->|"approve"| AUTO H -->|"reject"| X([Reject + reason]) H -->|"edit"| AUTO AUTO --> O([Execute])

2026 real-time scenario — Bank loan agent

A 2026 retail-bank loan copilot can auto-approve loans below ₹2L for prime customers. Above that, the agent prepares the file, runs underwriting checks, and routes to a human officer with a one-click "approve / reject / request docs" UI. SLA: human responds in < 4 hours.

Interview Q&A

How do you handle async human responses without blocking?
Persist the agent state, return a "pending" status, push a notification, and resume from a checkpoint when the human responds (LangGraph's checkpointer makes this trivial).
What's a good HITL UX?
Show the agent's reasoning + tool calls + risk flags. Provide three actions: approve / reject / edit. Capture the reason — it's a great fine-tuning signal.
