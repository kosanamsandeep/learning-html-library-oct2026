Chapter 21 — Exploration & Discovery

Don't only optimize known goals — sometimes you need to find the unknown unknowns.

StrategyVisitorCuriosity

What is it?

Most agents exploit — they pick the highest-known-reward action. Exploration introduces calculated randomness so the agent can find new options. Without it, an agent will keep recommending the same restaurant forever.

Mental model: a scout team in a strategy game — sent into fog of war so the rest of the army knows where to go.

2026 real-time scenario — Drug-discovery research agent

A 2026 pharma research agent has a goal "find anti-aging compounds." Pure exploitation reads only papers it already cites. Pure exploration is noise. ε-greedy with novelty bonus reads 80% on-topic + 20% adjacent fields (immunology, autophagy, mitochondrial biology) — exactly where the breakthroughs come from.

Interview Q&A

Why not always explore?
Pure exploration wastes budget on low-value paths. The point is balance: enough exploitation to deliver value now, enough exploration to avoid local minima.
Where does novelty come from in an LLM agent?
From an embedding distance to past visits, from low coverage of a topic in memory, or from explicit curiosity rewards (e.g., prediction error of a small model).
