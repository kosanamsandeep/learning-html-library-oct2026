Chapter 14 — Knowledge Retrieval (RAG)

Look it up before you answer. Ground the LLM in your private + current data.

RAGVector DBRepository

What is it?

The LLM was trained months ago and doesn't know your company's policies. RAG = retrieve relevant chunks from a knowledge base, stuff them into the prompt, generate a grounded answer.

Mental model: a lawyer who looks up case law before answering — never relies on memory alone.

Interview Q&A

Why does RAG hallucinate?
Common causes: bad retrieval (wrong chunks), prompt that allows fallback to model knowledge, no instruction to cite. Fix with reranking, "answer only from context or say I don't know," and citation enforcement.
What's good chunking?
Sentence- or paragraph-aware with overlap (~10-20%). Avoid chunks < 100 tokens (loss of context) and > 800 (precision loss). Add metadata for filtering (doc id, section, date).
