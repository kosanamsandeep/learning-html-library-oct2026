Why This Matters

LangGraph transforms how you build AI applications. Instead of simple linear chains that forget everything between steps, LangGraph gives you stateful, controllable, iterative workflows — the foundation of real AI agents. Each task in this series introduces a new superpower, building on the last until you have a complete research assistant.
