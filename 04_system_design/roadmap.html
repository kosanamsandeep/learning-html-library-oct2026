30-Day System Design Roadmap

Two weeks of LLD, two weeks of HLD. Each day = one focused reading + one whiteboard exercise. By Day 30 you should be able to walk into a product-company round and design any of the canonical systems out loud.

Week 1 — LLD Foundations

Day Topic Action
1 What is System Design? HLD vs LLD Read; sketch the mental map.
2 OOP fundamentals Refactor one class from 07_learning_projects/p2_banking_system using encapsulation.
3 SOLID — SRP & OCP Pick a violating class in your repo; refactor.
4 SOLID — LSP, ISP, DIP Identify a fat interface; split it.
5 Creational patterns Implement Factory + Builder for a Pizza class.
6 Structural patterns Implement Adapter + Decorator for a logger.
7 Behavioral patterns Implement Strategy + Observer for a payment flow.

Week 2 — LLD Case Studies

Day Topic Action
8 UML class & sequence diagrams Draw the diagram for your Day-7 payment flow.
9 Parking Lot Identify classes; code skeleton in Python.
10 Elevator System Model state machine; multiple elevators.
11 Library Management Member, Book, Loan; reservations.
12 ATM Machine Hardware abstraction + state machine.
13 Free practice Time-box: design a Splitwise in 45 min. Compare to standard answer online.
14 Free practice Time-box: design a Snake-and-Ladders game in 30 min.

Week 3 — HLD Primitives

Day Topic Action
15 HLD Fundamentals Memorize the back-of-envelope numbers table.
16 Vertical vs Horizontal Scaling Sketch a stateless web tier.
17 SQL vs NoSQL Pick the right DB for 3 scenarios.
18 Sharding & Replication Diagram a 4-shard MySQL cluster with read replicas.
19 Caching strategies Diagram cache-aside vs write-through for a product catalog.
20 Load Balancers L4 vs L7; sticky sessions; health checks.
21 Message Queues Sync vs async; Kafka vs RabbitMQ vs SQS.

Week 4 — HLD Case Studies

Day Topic Action
22 Microservices & API Gateway Identify service boundaries for an e-commerce app.
23 CAP theorem & consistency Classify 5 real systems as CP or AP.
24 URL Shortener (TinyURL) Design in 45 min; compare to standard answer.
25 Twitter Newsfeed Push vs pull model; fan-out problem.
26 WhatsApp Chat Online presence; last-seen; group chat.
27 Uber Dispatch Geo-sharding; driver matching.
28 YouTube Video encoding pipeline; CDN; recommendation pipeline.
29 Mock interview Pick a system you haven't designed; 45-min mock.
30 Reflect What can you design confidently? What still wobbles?
