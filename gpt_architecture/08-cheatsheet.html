📐 The Core Attention Equation

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( Q · KT / √dk ) · V
SymbolMeaningShape
QQuery — "what am I looking for?"(T × dk)
KKey — "what do I contain?"(T × dk)
VValue — "what information do I provide?"(T × dk)
TNumber of tokens in the sequencescalar
dkDimension per attention headscalar (typically 64–128)
n_embdFull embedding dimensionscalar (768–16000)
hNumber of attention headsscalar (12–128)

Multi-Head Extension

// Each head i has its own Q_i, K_i, V_i headi = Attention(X·WQi, X·WKi, X·WVi) // Concatenate all heads and project back MultiHead(X) = Concat(head1, ..., headh) · WO // Where W_O: (n_embd × n_embd), and each head output: (T × d_k) // d_k = n_embd / h — so concatenation recovers n_embd dimensions

📚 Key Terminology

Core Concepts

AttentionMechanism for tokens to gather context from other tokens via weighted blending
Self-attentionTokens attending to other tokens within the same sequence
Cross-attentionTokens in one sequence attending to tokens in another (encoder-decoder)
Causal / MaskedDecoder-only: tokens cannot attend to future tokens
Multi-headMultiple parallel attention "perspectives" (syntax, semantics, etc.)

Training Concepts

Cross-Entropy Loss-log P(actual_token) — how surprised the model was
BackpropagationGradient of loss flows backward through QKV operations
Gradient DescentW ← W - η · ∇W — update weights opposite gradient
LogitsRaw scores before softmax (output of unembedding)
TemperatureSharpens or flattens the softmax distribution at inference

🧠 Intuition Summary

The Three Roles in One Sentence Each:

Query"I am looking for specific information — let me scan all Keys to find it."
Key"Here's what I contain — if your Query matches me, pay attention to my Value."
Value"Here's my actual content — blend me into your representation proportionally to how much attention I received."

The complete equation is learnable, parallelizable, and content-addressable. Every parameter (WQ, WK, WV, WO) is updated by gradient descent to minimize the loss — which means the model discovers for itself which tokens should attend to which other tokens, for any given task.
