01 · What Is Fine-Tuning?

Before learning how LoRA and QLoRA work, we need to understand why fine-tuning exists and what problem it solves.

🧠 The Pretrain-Then-Adapt Paradigm

Modern LLMs are pretrained on internet-scale data (trillions of tokens) to predict the next token. This gives them broad knowledge of language, facts, and reasoning patterns. But a base pretrained model is not useful out of the box — it just completes text.

Fine-tuning is the process of taking that pretrained model and adapting it to a specific task, style, or domain by continuing training on a smaller, targeted dataset.

🎯 The key insight: Pretraining teaches the model language. Fine-tuning teaches the model what to do with it — follow instructions, chat, write code, summarize, etc.

💡 Why Not Just Prompt Engineer?

Prompt engineering works for many tasks, but has hard limits:

  • Context window bound — you can't fit an entire domain's knowledge into a prompt
  • No memory — the model doesn't "learn" from your prompts; every call is fresh
  • Fragile — small prompt changes can cause large output changes
  • No structural change — you can't teach the model a new format or behavior that's very different from its pretraining distribution
⚡ Fine-tuning baked the behavior into the weights. An instruction-tuned model (like Llama-2-Chat or Mistral-Instruct) was fine-tuned on instruction-response pairs. The model isn't told to follow instructions — it simply does, because the weights have been updated to produce instruction-following behavior.

💡 PEFT is the default choice in 2024+. Unless you have a very specific need to update all weights (and the budget for it), start with LoRA or QLoRA. They cover 95% of practical fine-tuning use cases.

🔑 Before We Dive In

Throughout this series, a few themes will recur:

  • Memory cost — the biggest bottleneck in fine-tuning. Storing optimizer states (Adam: 2× model size in floats) often costs more than the weights themselves.
  • Catastrophic forgetting — updating too many weights can erase the pretrained knowledge. PEFT avoids this by keeping base weights frozen.
  • Multi-task serving — PEFT adapters can be loaded and unloaded dynamically, serving hundreds of fine-tuned variants from one base model.
