The Elicitation Era: Opus 5 & the New Claude Code

Boris Cherny — head of Claude Code at Anthropic — on why frontier models now run for months, why he deletes 80% of system prompts, and why the senior engineer's job is shifting from context engineering to task elicitation.

Source: Boris Cherny discussion around the Claude Opus 5 release + Claude Code (verified speaker). Compiled 2026-08-03.
⚡ The one-line thesis: models have outgrown their harnesses. Your job is no longer to constrain the model with detailed instructions — it's to elicit latent capability by giving it a clear task, guardrails, exit criteria, and a way to verify its own work. Everything below is a concrete practice built on that thesis.

Learning Path — 5 Modules

01

Press Delete: The Ablation Mindset

Cherny deletes 80% of the system prompt with each model generation. System prompts, skills, hooks — ablate them line-by-line. Re-add only when the model stumbles. `claude_code_simple=1` for internal ablations.

Learn: ablation practice · treating the model as a co-worker · every-six-month reset
02

Verification Loops & Goal-Oriented Prompting

The single most important thing devs get wrong: not letting the AI verify its own work. Define task, guardrails, exit criteria — not the step-by-step how. Long-running agents drift; give them tangible feedback to self-correct.

Learn: exit criteria · guardrails · self-verification · the "ignorant advantage"
03

Dynamic Workflows & Massive Orchestration

Opus 5 can orchestrate thousands–tens of thousands of agents — a 100K-line Zig→Rust rewrite in 11 days. The "agentic algebra": sequence + parallelise + fan-out + verify batches. Test-time compute scales with task stakes.

Learn: agentic algebra · fan-out/verify patterns · test-time compute economics
04

Self-Maintaining Infrastructure

Loops (local) and Routines (cloud) let Claude maintain itself: the "abstraction police" unifying duplicated code, routines deleting dead code, shipping experiments. The engineering drudgery becomes autonomous.

Learn: loop vs routine · abstraction police · autonomous tech-debt cleanup
05

Security, Overhang & the Human Moat

Opus 5's three-layer injection defense you can replicate. Product overhang vs underhang — diagnose which you're fighting. And the human moat: architecture & design sense that agents can't automate.

Learn: 3-layer defense · overhang/underhang diagnosis · the non-automatable role
