MethodCore ideaReward model?Notes

RLAIFSame, but an AI labels preferences instead of humansYes (AI-labeled)Scales labelling; slightly noisier

Instruction TuningSupervised fine-tuning on instruction→response pairsNoThe base layer of "chat" models
Why GRPO matters to you: it's the RL method behind DeepSeek's reasoning models. Understanding it explains why DeepSeek models are both strong at reasoning and cheap to serve — group-relative advantage removes the memory-hungry critic network that classic PPO/RLHF needs. Go to the DeepSeekMath GRPO paper for implementation detail.

How Fine-Tuning Fits With Your Projects

When to reach for PEFT

  • Consistent output format (JSON, schemas)
  • Domain style / vocabulary
  • Reduce prompt length by baking behaviour into weights
  • You have open-source weights to download

When not to (from your own learnings)

  • Frontier API models (DeepSeek, Claude) — no weights, API fine-tuning is expensive
  • Dynamic knowledge → use RAG instead
  • Fewer than ~100 clean examples → prompt engineering first
Bottom line: for your single-user learning setups, QLoRA on a 7–13B open model is the practical tool. Understanding GRPO/DPO gives you the conceptual layer behind why those models behave the way they do — which is exactly the depth worth having when you benchmark DeepSeek against GLM/Kimi.
