OpenAI's Postgres Architecture — 800M Users Verified

Verified real — OpenAI's own engineering blog ("Scaling PostgreSQL to power 800 million ChatGPT users"). The headline numbers people kept sharing are accurate:

  • Roughly 800M weekly active users served by one Azure Postgres primary + ~50 read replicas.
  • PgBouncer for connection pooling (many connections multiplexed onto few actual connections).
  • Cache locking to keep the hot cache coherent across replicas.
  • WAL fan-out (the log shipping that feeds replicas) is the key scaling constraint.
  • Actively exploring cascading read replicas (replicas of replicas) to fan out further.
The takeaway that surprises people: they deliberately avoided sharding. One beefy primary + many replicas was the right call at this scale because of read-heavy, single-key workloads. Sharding adds enormous operational complexity; it's only worth it when writes or size genuinely exceed one primary. This is a masterclass in "choose the simplest architecture that meets the requirement."
